Ribat - built-in adhan recordings: sources and processing
Updated: 2026-10-13 (v1.6.0+). 14 recordings supplied by the app owner, who states they are openly licensed.

Processing (all files):
  Android res/raw/adhan_<id>.mp3: silence trimmed (-45 dB), two-pass EBU R128 loudnorm I=-16 LUFS TP=-1.5,
    mono; 16/22 kHz sources -> 22.05 kHz 32 kbps, 44.1 kHz sources -> 32 kHz 56 kbps, metadata stripped. Full adhan.
  assets/sounds/adhan_<id>.mp3: first 30 s of the above with 2 s fade (in-app preview only).
  assets/sounds/ios/ and ios/Runner/Sounds/adhan_<id>.caf: first 29 s, 1.5 s fade, mono 22.05 kHz IMA4 (iOS limit 30 s).
